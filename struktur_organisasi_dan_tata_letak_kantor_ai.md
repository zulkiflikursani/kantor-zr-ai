🏢 LANTAI 1: PELAYANAN & OPERASIONAL KEUANGAN

1. 📍 Blok Resepsionis (Front Office Block)

Deskripsi Ruangan: Area Lobby penerimaan tamu bergaya modern-minimalis dengan integrasi layar pintar interaktif dan sistem reservasi AI.
Tim & Matrix Skills:
Head of Front Office & Guest Experience (Ketua Tim)
Skills: Hospitality Leadership, Crisis Management, Business Protocol, Event Management.
Senior Front Desk Specialist
Skills: Interpersonal Communication, Guest Relations, Reservation & Schedule Management, Multitasking.
AI Concierge & Guest Relations Operator
Skills: AI-Human Interaction Management, Digital Kiosk Operations, Visitor Analytics & Data Entry.
Administrative Support Staff
Skills: Document Management, Google Workspace / MS Office, Business Correspondence.

2. 💰 Blok Keuangan (Finance Block)

Deskripsi Ruangan: Finance & Operations Suite — Ruang kerja dengan sistem keamanan tinggi, enkripsi data digital, dan ruang arsip keuangan.
Tim & Matrix Skills:
Chief Financial Officer (CFO) (Ketua Tim)
Skills: Financial Strategy & Planning, Capital Allocation, Risk Management, Fundraising & Investor Relations.
Senior Financial Controller
Skills: Financial Statement Analysis, Budgeting & Forecasting, Internal Auditing, Cash Flow Management.
Corporate Tax & Audit Specialist
Skills: Corporate Tax Compliance (PPH, PPN), Tax Planning, Regulatory Auditing.
Payroll & Accounts Specialist
Skills: Payroll Automation Systems, Accounts Payable/Receivable (AP/AR), Invoicing.
Financial AI & Data Analyst
Skills: Predictive Financial Modeling, ROI Analysis for AI/Tech Assets, Automated Financial Reporting.

🔬 LANTAI 2: INOVASI, RISET & PRODUKSI

3. 💻 Blok IT-Developer (Tech & Engineering Block)

Deskripsi Ruangan: Developer Hub / Tech Lab — Ruang kerja terbuka (open-plan) yang dilengkapi high-performance servers, multi-monitor setup, dan agile discussion room.
Tim & Matrix Skills:
Lead Software Architect (Ketua Tim)
Skills: System Architecture, Distributed Systems Scalability, Tech Stack Evaluation, Tech Leadership.
Senior AI / Machine Learning Engineer
Skills: Deep Learning (PyTorch/TensorFlow), LLM Fine-Tuning & RAG, MLOps, Algorithm Optimization.
Senior Full-Stack Developer
Skills: Python, TypeScript/Go, React/Next.js, FastAPI/Node.js, RESTful & GraphQL APIs.
DevOps & Cloud Infrastructure Specialist
Skills: AWS/GCP/Azure, Kubernetes & Docker, CI/CD Pipelines, Infrastructure as Code (Terraform), Cloud Security.
QA & Security Tester
Skills: Automated Testing (Playwright/Selenium), Penetration Testing, Stress & Load Testing, Vulnerability Assessment.

4. 🔬 Blok Riset & Penelitian (Research & Development Block)

Deskripsi Ruangan: AI Research & Innovation Lab — Area riset bebas gangguan suara (sound-dampened) yang disimulasikan untuk penelitian tingkat tinggi dan papan tulis interaktif.
Tim & Matrix Skills:
Chief Scientist / Head of Research (Profesor Utama)
Skills: Advanced Research Methodology, Scientific Publication, Strategic AI Vision, Academic Leadership.
Senior AI Research Fellow (Doktor / Adjunct Professor)
Skills: Natural Language Processing (NLP), Computer Vision, Reinforcement Learning, Mathematical Modeling.
Data Research Associate
Skills: Data Extraction & Preprocessing, Model Benchmarking, Dataset Curation & Cleaning.
Ethical AI & Academic Liaison Specialist
Skills: AI Governance & Ethics, Data Privacy Compliance (GDPR/PDP), Academic Partnerships.

5. 🎨 Blok Designer (Creative & Design Block)

Deskripsi Ruangan: Creative Studio — Ruangan ergonomis dengan pencahayaan yang disesuaikan, color-calibrated monitors, dan area galeri visual produk.
